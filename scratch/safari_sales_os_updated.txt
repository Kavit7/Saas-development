1. CORE PROBLEM STATEMENT
The Problem You're Solving
As a sales person in the tourism industry:










You book safaris for clients
Reservation manager books accommodations
You receive multiple invoices (one per accommodation)
Each invoice has an expiration date
With many clients, you can't remember all deadlines
Missing a payment means accommodation cancels
Reservation manager can't track for entire sales team
Accommodation requests sometimes get missed or forgotten
No system to track what's confirmed vs. what's missing

Vision
A system that:








Tracks all invoice deadlines and reminds you 3 days before
Creates client files with invoices and special requirements
Tracks dietary restrictions and accommodation needs
Helps you remember what you've booked vs. what's missing
Works for your whole team
Can eventually be sold to other tour companies
